About PolitíCat

"Curiosity > Politics."

PolitíCat NM is a civic education brand built on one idea: when people understand how government actually works, everyone wins.

Don Gato Cívico is our host: curious, skeptical, and allergic to spin. He does not tell you what to think. He helps you understand, then points you to the real source so you can decide for yourself.
