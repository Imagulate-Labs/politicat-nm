JUDICIAL RETENTION Shall Judge [NAME PENDING] of the Court of Appeals be retained in office?
⚠ Overvote — mark only YES or NO.
🐱 Learn: What is judicial retention? · Sources
Retention isn't judge vs. judge — it's a yes/no on keeping a sitting judge. In New Mexico a judge needs 57% YES to stay (that's in the state constitution). The Judicial Performance Evaluation Commission publishes evaluations. Read the JPEC evaluations ↗
BOND QUESTION Sample: General obligation bond for library facilities
⚠ Overvote — mark only FOR or AGAINST.
🐱 Learn: What happens if this passes? What stays the same? · Sources
If it passes: the state may borrow the stated amount for the stated purpose, repaid through existing property-tax levies. What stays the same: your tax rate doesn't automatically change — bonds are structured within current levies. Who carries it out: the agencies named in the bond act. No score, no recommendation — just what the record says. SOS ballot information ↗
